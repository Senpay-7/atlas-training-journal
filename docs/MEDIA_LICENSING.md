# Media and content rights

No external exercise photo, demonstration video, creator portrait, paid manual or anatomical artwork is bundled in this milestone. UI iconography is supplied by `@expo/vector-icons` / Ionicons under its upstream notices. Gradients and decorative geometry are authored in the app. The exercise pictogram is not an anatomical demonstration.

For every future media record require: stable ID, source page and asset URL, author, license identifier and URL, permission evidence, attribution text, local file name, MIME type, dimensions, SHA-256, checked date, modification/crop notes, offline redistribution and commercial-use decisions. Store each image/video's terms separately from text and software licenses.

Free Exercise DB's repository-level Unlicense does not settle third-party image provenance. wger records require individual license/author/source review; fulfill applicable attribution and share-alike terms for reused or adapted content. Reference links do not authorize downloading and redistributing creator videos. Brand names and logos must not imply endorsement.

Future exercise artwork must be checked for movement accuracy and accessibility, regardless of whether it is licensed, drawn or generated. Profile images stay local and must be included in a future portable asset backup; a URI alone is not a portable backup.
